About

Hewang Nie is a Faculty Postdoctoral Fellow with a Ph.D. in Cyberspace Security from Huazhong University of Science and Technology. He received his doctoral degree in June 2025. His research focuses on AI security, reversible information hiding, and neural network watermarking.

He leads a Young Scientists Fund project of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and has led a student innovation funding project from the Cyberspace Administration of China. He has also participated in the National Key R&D Program of China. He has published over ten papers in well-known journals and conferences, including 6 papers in SCI Q1 TOP journals and 10 CCF-A/B or T1 high-level papers. He holds 3 invention patents and 3 software copyrights.
